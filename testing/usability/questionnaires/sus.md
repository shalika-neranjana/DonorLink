## Scoring (Brooke, 1996; report Appendix C.6)

1. Odd items (1, 3, 5, 7, 9): contribution = **score − 1**.
2. Even items (2, 4, 6, 8, 10): contribution = **5 − score**.
3. Add the ten contributions (0-40) and multiply by **2.5** → **0-100**.

Worked check (arithmetic only, not data): all answers 3 → odd 2 each (×5 = 10), even 2 each (×5 = 10) → 20 × 2.5 = **50**. All best answers (odd 5, even 1) → 40 × 2.5 = **100**.

* SUS is **not** a percentage and **not** the mean of the raw answers.
* A questionnaire with a missing item is not scored (the workbook leaves it blank and excludes it from the mean).
* Report each participant's score, the mean, the range and the number of valid questionnaires. About 68 is a commonly cited average (Sauro & Lewis, report ref. [5]); with fewer than 10 participants treat it as indicative only.
