# Participant briefing and consent

## Read-aloud consent script (PDF Appendix C.1)

> "Thank you for helping us. We are testing the DonorLink app, not you, so there are no wrong answers. Please think aloud while you use it. The session takes about 20 minutes. You will use a test account with made-up information. Please do not enter any real personal or medical details. We will take notes and, only if you agree, record the screen. You can stop at any time without giving a reason. Your answers will be reported anonymously as a participant number."
>
> **Question 1:** "Do you agree to take part?"
> **Question 2:** "Do you agree to screen recording?"

Record: participant ID, consent (yes/no), recording consent (yes/no), date. Verbal consent is enough (report §9.2); a signed copy of the form below is also acceptable.

## What participants are and aren't told

* Told: purpose, length, think-aloud, test account, they may stop, anonymity, recording choice.
* Not told in advance: the six tasks, where features are, what other participants did.
* Not collected: name, phone, e-mail, address, photo, face on video, real medical information.

## Data handling

* Notes and workbooks use the participant ID only. The mapping from person to ID is not recorded.
* Recordings (if consented) stay on a project-owned drive with restricted access; they are not committed to git (`results/private/` is git-ignored).
* Screenshots shown in reports contain only synthetic data and no faces.
