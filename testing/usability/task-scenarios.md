# Task scenario cards (participant-facing)

Print one card per page, or read it aloud. **Read the card only.** Do not name screens, buttons, tabs or fields. The wording follows Table 15 of the report (abridged there) and keeps its meaning.

Tell the participant: *"Please say out loud what you are thinking. I can't help with how to do it, but if you are stuck I may give one small hint."*

Start the timer when you finish reading the card.

---

## Card T1: Sign in

> You are new to DonorLink. Sign in with the test account you were given.

*(Hand over the card with the test account details written on it. Return the card at the end; the card is not kept in the repository.)*

---

## Card T2: Ask for blood

> Your uncle needs 2 units of O+ blood urgently at a hospital in Colombo. Ask for help using the app.

---

## Card T3: Check your request

> Find out whether your request has been verified and how many donors have responded.

*(Ask the participant to say the answer aloud.)*

---

## Card T4: Tell the app when you can donate

> You can donate today until 6 pm. Make sure the app knows this.

*(Facilitator: if the session is after 18:00, replace "6 pm" with a time later than now and note the wording used on the recording sheet.)*

---

## Card T5: Answer an alert

> You received an alert asking for your blood group. Look at it and agree to donate.

---

## Card T6: Find your history

> Find your donation and request history.

*(Ask the participant to read out one past record.)*

---

### Hint rule (all cards)

If the participant is stuck for **60 seconds** or asks for help **twice**: give **one neutral hint** (for example "What else could you try on this screen?"), record an *assist*. Never name the control. A task given up, or not finished within **3 minutes**, is *abandoned*; move on.
